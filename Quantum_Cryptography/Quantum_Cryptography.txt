Quantum cryptography uses principles of quantum mechanics to secure information.

Key features:
- Quantum key distribution (QKD) allows two parties to share a secret key with detection of eavesdropping.
- Quantum states such as photons are used to transmit bits in a way that any interception disturbs the state.
- Security is based on physics rather than computational assumptions.

Applications include secure communications, encryption key exchange, and protection against future quantum computer attacks.
